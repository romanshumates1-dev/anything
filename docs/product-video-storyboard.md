| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|

### Scene 1: The Problem Hook
**Timestamp**: 0:00 - 0:05 (5 seconds)

#### Visual Description
Fast-paced montage creating immediate tension and relatability:
1. **Shot 1 (0.8s)**: Close-up of hands frantically scrolling through an overwhelming Excel spreadsheet with 500+ rows of leads
2. **Shot 2 (0.8s)**: Phone screen showing 47 unread text messages, notification badges stacking
3. **Shot 3 (0.8s)**: Missed calls counter animating from 12 to 23 in rapid succession
4. **Shot 4 (0.8s)**: Desktop showing 15+ browser tabs, email inbox with 200+ unread
5. **Shot 5 (0.8s)**: Full screen crack effect transitioning out - glass shattering from center

#### Motion Graphics Specs
- **Transition Style**: Rapid hard cuts (0.8s each), no crossfades
- **Color Treatment**: Desaturated, slightly orange/warm tint to feel dated
- **Screen Crack Effect**: Vector-based crack animation starting from center, glass shards flying outward with motion blur
- **Camera Movement**: Slight shake/handheld feel to emphasize chaos

#### Text Overlay
| Element | Text | Position | Animation |
|---------|------|----------|-----------|
| Main Text | "Still stuck in the stone age?" | Center screen, appears after crack | Glitch effect in, holds 1.5s |

- **Font**: Inter Bold, 72pt
- **Color**: White with red tint (#ff4444)
- **Shadow**: 4px drop shadow, 50% opacity black

#### Voiceover Script
> "Still doing wholesaling like it's 2015?"

**Delivery Notes**: Slightly accusatory but friendly tone. Quick delivery, 1.5 seconds.

#### Music Cue
- Low synth pulse, tension building
- 808 sub-bass undertone
- Cuts abruptly at 0:05 with the crack effect

## 4. PRODUCTION NOTES

### Recommended Software

**Video Editing**:
- **Primary**: Adobe Premiere Pro 2024
  - Timeline editing, color grading, audio mixing
  - Best for screen recording assembly
- **Alternative**: DaVinci Resolve (free tier sufficient)

**Motion Graphics**:
- **Primary**: Adobe After Effects 2024
  - Logo animation, 3-step workflow, all motion graphics
  - Recommended plugins: Motion v3, Flow, BRAW Toolbox
- **Alternative**: Apple Motion (Mac only)

**Screen Recording**:
- **Primary**: ScreenFlow (Mac) or Camtasia (Windows)
- **Browser Extension**: Loom (for quick captures)
- **Settings**: 1920x1080, 60fps, no compression

**Audio**:
- **Voiceover Recording**: Adobe Audition or Audacity
- **Music Editing**: Adobe Audition
- **AI Voiceover Option**: ElevenLabs or Descript

**Collaboration**:
- **Script/Storyboard Review**: Frame.io or Vimeo Review
