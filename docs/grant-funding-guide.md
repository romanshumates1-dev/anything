## TIER 1: DIRECT APPLICATION - HIGHEST PRIORITY

### 1. NSF SBIR/STTR (Apply Immediately)

**The single best funding source for AI startups. Apply directly, no consortium needed.**

| Phase | Amount | Timeline | Purpose |
|-------|--------|----------|---------|
| Phase I | Up to $323,090 | 6-12 months | Proof of concept |
| Phase II | Up to $2,153,927 | 24 months | Full development |
| Phase III | Unlimited | Ongoing | Commercialization (agency contracts) |

**Why NSF is Best for DealFlow AI:**
- Technology-agnostic: "Almost all areas of technology and market sectors"
- AI explicitly funded
- Rolling submissions (no deadline)
- No consortium required
- Entry point: seedfund.nsf.gov

**Application Strategy:**
1. Submit Project Pitch (2-3 pages) via seedfund.nsf.gov
2. NSF responds within 3 weeks with invitation to apply or feedback
3. If invited, submit full Phase I proposal
4. Award decision in ~6 months

## TIER 2: STATE MEGA-GRANTS ($1M-$50M)

### Texas Programs

**Texas Enterprise Fund (TEF)**
- **Amount:** $5M-$40M+ (Apple got $21M for 3,600 jobs)
- **Requirements:** 75 jobs (urban) or 25 jobs (rural), competitive out-of-state bid
- **Application:** TEF Portal, unanimous approval from Governor, Lt. Gov, Speaker
- **Best For:** HQ relocation with job creation commitments

**Texas JETI (Jobs, Energy, Technology, Innovation)**
- **Amount:** For $50M+ capital investment projects
- **Requirements:** New capital investment + high-paying jobs
- **Best For:** Later-stage with significant infrastructure needs

### Ohio Programs

**JobsOhio Growth Fund**
- **Amount:** Flexible, project-based (no rigid dollar-per-job formula)
- **Requirements:** Must generate 50%+ revenue outside Ohio
- **Best For:** SaaS companies with national customer base
- **Contact:** (614) 224-6446

**Ohio Third Frontier**
- **Amount:** $50K-$500K technology commercialization grants
- **Programs:** Technology Validation, Entrepreneurial Services
- **Best For:** Early-stage tech companies

### North Carolina Programs

**Job Development Investment Grant (JDIG)**
- **Amount:** 75-100% of employee income tax withholdings for 12-30 years
- **Requirements by Tier:**

| Category | Jobs | Investment | Duration |
|----------|------|------------|----------|
| Standard | Varies | Varies | 12 years |
| High-Yield | 1,750 | $500M | 20 years |
| Transformative | 3,000 | $1B | 30+ years |

**One NC Fund**
- **Amount:** Discretionary cash grants
- **Requirements:** Job creation + local match
- **Local Match Ratios:** Tier 1: $1:$3, Tier 2: $1:$2, Tier 3: $1:$1

### Tennessee Programs (BEST FOR EARLY-STAGE)

**FastTrack Programs**
1. Job Training Assistance - Fund employee training
2. Economic Development Fund - Real property/construction
3. Infrastructure Program - Public infrastructure (requires local match)

**Enhanced Job Tax Credit**
- **Amount:** $4,500/job annually for 3-5 years
- **Requirements:**

| Tier | Jobs Required | Investment | Duration |
|------|---------------|------------|----------|
| Tier 2 | 25+ | $500K+ | 3 years |
| Tier 3 | 20+ | $500K+ | 5 years |
| Tier 4 | 10+ | $500K+ | 5 years |

**HQ Tax Credit**
- **Benefit:** 6.5% sales tax credit on qualified property
- **Requirements:** $10M+ investment AND 100+ positions at 150% state avg wage

**Why Tennessee is Ideal for DealFlow:**
- Lowest thresholds (10 jobs, $500K investment)
- No state income tax
- $4,500/job credit = $45K/year for 10 employees
- FastTrack bonuses: +35% at-risk counties, +50% distressed counties

### Nevada Programs

**Tax Abatement Programs**
- **$1B Investment Tier:**
  - Sales/use tax: 4.6%-6.125% reduction for 15 years
  - Modified Business Tax: 75% reduction for 10 years
  - Property taxes: 75% reduction for 10 years

- **$3.5B Investment Tier:**
  - Sales/use tax: 4.6%-6.125% reduction for 20 years
  - Modified Business Tax: 100% reduction for 10 years
  - Property taxes: 100% reduction for 10 years

**Best For:** Companies with out-of-state revenue (SaaS fits perfectly)
**Contact:** GOED (800) 336-1600

### Virginia Programs

**VEDIG (Economic Development Incentive Grant)**
- **Focus:** HQ, administrative, service sector
- **Amount:** Negotiated, competitive projects only
- **Contact:** VEDP (804) 545-5600

**VJIP (Jobs Investment Program)**
- **Amount:** $500-$1,000 per eligible job
- **Purpose:** Training/recruiting reimbursement

## KEY INSIGHTS

### The 25-50x Leverage Math

**VC Route:**
- $1M at $10M pre = 10% dilution
- At exit ($100M), that 10% = $10M in foregone equity

**Grant Route:**
- $5M in grants = 0% dilution
- At exit ($100M), you keep 100% = $100M vs $90M
- **Net benefit: $10M+ in preserved equity**

### Why This Works for DealFlow AI

1. **AI/Tech focus** - NSF SBIR explicitly funds AI
2. **SaaS model** - Out-of-state revenue qualifies for multiple state programs
3. **Real estate angle** - HUD and housing programs available
4. **Job creation** - Even 10-25 jobs unlocks significant incentives
5. **Relocation flexibility** - Can negotiate between states

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not applying to SBIR** - Highest ROI, lowest barrier
2. **Ignoring state matches** - Free money for same work
3. **Single-state focus** - Create competitive bidding
4. **Skipping cloud credits** - $150K+ available immediately
5. **Missing deadlines** - Track annual cycles (43North, MassChallenge)
