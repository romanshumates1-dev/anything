# Acceptable Use & Messaging Policy

This policy governs your use of {{LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME}}'s messaging features and mirrors carrier and CTIA requirements. Violation may result in immediate suspension.

## 1. Consent Is Required

You must have prior express consent to message every recipient before importing or contacting them. **Purchased or scraped lists are prohibited.** You must be able to produce proof of consent on request.

## 2. Prohibited Content (SHAFT)

Messages must not contain content relating to Sex, Hate, Alcohol, Firearms, or Tobacco, nor any unlawful, deceptive, or high-risk financial content prohibited by carriers.

## 3. Mandatory STOP / HELP Handling

- **STOP** (and equivalents) immediately and permanently adds the number to the suppression list; no further messages may be sent to it.
- **HELP** returns program information and support contact.

These behaviors are enforced by the platform server-side and may not be disabled.

## 4. Quiet Hours

Messages are only sent between 8:00 AM and 9:00 PM in the recipient's local time zone. Messages scheduled outside that window are automatically deferred.

## 5. No Spam / Volume Abuse

Do not send unsolicited bulk messages, evade opt-outs, or otherwise abuse the platform. Excessive complaints or opt-out rates may trigger review and suspension.

## 6. Enforcement

We may suspend or terminate accounts that violate this policy. Repeated or egregious violations result in permanent termination. Questions: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}
