## Filing a Notice

A valid notice must include: identification of the copyrighted work; identification of the allegedly infringing material and its location; your contact information; a statement of good-faith belief; a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and you are authorized to act; and your physical or electronic signature.

## Counter-Notice

If your material was removed in error, you may submit a counter-notice with the equivalent statutory elements.
