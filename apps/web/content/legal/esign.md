# Electronic Records & Signature Consent (E-SIGN / UETA)

This disclosure is presented, and your affirmative consent is recorded, **before** you enter any electronic-signature flow on the platform operated by {{LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME}}.

## Consent to Electronic Records

By affirmatively consenting, you agree that electronic signatures and records have the same legal effect as handwritten signatures and paper documents under the federal E-SIGN Act and applicable state UETA.

## Hardware & Software Requirements

To access and retain electronic records you need an internet-connected device, a current web browser, a valid email address, and the ability to view and download PDF documents.

## Withdrawing Consent

You may withdraw consent to electronic records by contacting {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Withdrawal does not invalidate documents you already signed electronically.

## Retaining Copies

We recommend downloading and saving every signed document. Copies remain available in your account while it is active.
