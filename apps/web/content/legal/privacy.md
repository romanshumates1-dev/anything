# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how {{LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME}} ("we," "us," "Company") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use our platform. This policy applies to all users of our website and services.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide

- **Account information:** Name, email address, phone number, organization name, and billing details
- **Lead and contact data:** Names, phone numbers, addresses, and property information you import or create
- **Message content:** SMS, email, and other communications sent and received through the platform
- **Contract data:** Documents, signatures, and transaction details
- **Support communications:** Messages and files you share with our support team

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage data:** Pages visited, features used, actions taken, and timestamps
- **Device information:** Browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and IP address
- **Location data:** General location derived from IP address
- **Log data:** Server logs, error reports, and diagnostic information
- **Analytics data:** Aggregated usage statistics and performance metrics

### 1.3 Information from Third Parties

- **Property data providers:** Public records, property information, and ownership data
- **Payment processors:** Transaction details and billing information
- **Authentication providers:** Single sign-on information if used

## 2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- **Provide services:** Operate the platform, process messages, and execute features
- **Process payments:** Handle billing, invoices, and refunds
- **Enforce compliance:** Monitor for policy violations, manage opt-outs, and maintain suppression lists
- **Improve services:** Analyze usage patterns, debug issues, and develop new features
- **Communicate with you:** Send service notifications, updates, and respond to inquiries
- **Ensure security:** Detect fraud, prevent abuse, and protect our systems
- **Meet legal obligations:** Comply with laws, respond to legal requests, and enforce our terms

## 3. Third-Party Service Providers (Subprocessors)

We share data with service providers strictly to operate the platform. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data:

| Provider | Purpose | Data Processed |
|----------|---------|----------------|
| **Twilio** | SMS transport | Phone numbers, message content |
| **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** | Cloud infrastructure, email (SES), AI services (Bedrock) | All platform data |
| **Stripe** | Payment processing | Billing information, payment details |
| **Neon** | Database hosting | All stored data |
| **Anthropic** | AI model provider | Message content for AI features |

**We do not sell your personal information.** We do not share your contact lists or customer data with third parties for their marketing purposes.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain data as follows:

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Account information** | Until account deletion, plus legally required retention |
| **Message logs** | 7 years (TCPA compliance) |
| **Consent records** | 7 years minimum |
| **Financial records** | 7 years (tax and accounting requirements) |
| **Contract documents** | Duration of account plus 10 years |
| **Usage analytics** | 2 years (anonymized after) |
| **Support communications** | 3 years after resolution |

We may retain certain data longer if required by law or to resolve disputes.

## 5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

### 5.1 All Users

- **Access:** Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
- **Portability:** Receive your data in a machine-readable format
- **Objection:** Object to certain processing activities

### 5.2 California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), you have the right to:

- Know what personal information we collect and how it is used
- Request deletion of your personal information
- Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell your data)
- Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

**Categories of Personal Information Collected (last 12 months):**
- Identifiers (name, email, phone, IP address)
- Commercial information (transaction history, billing records)
- Internet activity (usage data, browsing history on our platform)
- Professional information (business name, role)
- Geolocation data (general location from IP)

### 5.3 EEA/UK Residents (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have additional rights:

- **Legal basis:** We process data based on contract performance, legitimate interests, consent, and legal obligations
- **Data transfers:** Data may be transferred outside the EEA under Standard Contractual Clauses
- **Right to lodge a complaint:** You may complain to your local supervisory authority

### 5.4 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any privacy right, contact us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with:

- Your full name and email address associated with your account
- A description of your request
- Any information to help us verify your identity

We will respond within 30 days (45 days for complex requests). We may request additional verification to protect your data.

## 6. SMS and Messaging Data

### 6.1 Consent Management

- Consent status is stored and tracked for each contact
- Opt-out (STOP) requests are processed immediately and permanently
- Suppression lists are maintained and enforced server-side
- We retain proof of consent as required by TCPA

### 6.2 Message Content

- Message content may be processed by AI for response generation and classification
- Messages are logged for compliance and dispute resolution
- You control the content of messages you send

See our SMS Program Terms and Acceptable Use Policy for additional details.

## 7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- **Encryption in transit:** TLS 1.3 for all data transmission
- **Encryption at rest:** AES-256 encryption for sensitive data
- **Access controls:** Role-based access, multi-factor authentication
- **Monitoring:** Continuous security monitoring and logging
- **Vendor security:** All subprocessors maintain SOC 2 compliance or equivalent

While we take security seriously, no system is 100% secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your account credentials.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

## 9. Do Not Track

We do not currently respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals as there is no industry standard for compliance.

## 10. International Data Transfers

If you access our services from outside the United States, your data will be transferred to and processed in the United States. We use Standard Contractual Clauses and other appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated via email or in-app notification at least 30 days before taking effect. The "Effective Date" at the top indicates the last revision.

Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
