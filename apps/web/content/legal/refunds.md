# Refund & Billing Policy

This policy states the billing and refund terms for {{LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME}}. The refund controls available to administrators in the platform enforce exactly the terms below.

## Billing

Subscriptions are billed monthly in advance through our payment processor. Plan prices are shown on the pricing page and are the single source of truth for what you are charged.

## Refund Window

**You may request a full refund of your most recent subscription charge within 7 days of that charge.** After 7 days, charges for the current period are non-refundable, though you may cancel to avoid future charges.

> Note for reviewers: this 7-day window is the single authoritative refund term. Any earlier "30-day money-back" language on the marketing site has been removed to eliminate the prior contradiction (BREAKAGE_TABLE #31). [FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW — confirm the final window before launch.]

## How to Request

Contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with your account email and reason. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method through our processor and are mirrored in your account's billing records. Refunds typically settle within 5–7 business days.

## Cancellation

You may cancel at any time from account settings. Cancellation stops future charges; it does not retroactively refund prior periods except as stated above.

## Chargebacks

If you dispute a charge with your bank rather than contacting us, we may suspend your account until the dispute is resolved.
